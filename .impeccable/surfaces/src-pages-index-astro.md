## Scope

Landing page (single route, `/`). Visitor mode: Persuade. Audience: people curious about a calm neighbourhood café, mostly on phones; portfolio reviewers. Action: contact the café (WhatsApp, Instagram, email — demonstration channels, visibly labelled). No prices, no ordering, no forms.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is Monika's painted shopfront and the room behind it: a sign-written fascia, window bays that open like a shutter, a menu board on the wall, the counter, the room, the door. It refuses the category default of a cream page with a split hero, a three-card feature row and a uniform product grid.

OWN-WORLD: Deep bottle-green paint (#213A2E) carries the façade, the menu board, the door and the footer; gilt (#D2AE62) with a dark sign-writer's shade is reserved for lettering on green. Inside, plaster cream (#F5EFE6), warm white and oat, espresso-brown ink. Besley (Clarendon) for sign letters and headings, Yellowtail only for the "Monika's" signature, Libre Franklin for text. Thin gilt rules with notched corners frame boards. Square-cornered photographs in green joinery.

STORY: In the first seconds the visitor reads the name, sees coffee and the room, and finds "Escríbenos". Scrolling walks them in: what the house is, the full menu on one board, how one cup is made (with real measurements), what the room feels like, and the door: three ways to ask about prices or availability.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top: slim green bar with the wordmark, five links and "Escríbenos". Then the fascia: "Monika's" in gilt script about 9rem wide-set, "COFFEE" in spaced Besley caps, a one-line descriptor. Below, two window bays in green joinery: a wide golden interior (left, ~2/3) and a tall door pane with latte art (right). A green stallriser under the windows holds one sentence and two actions: "Escríbenos" (primary) and "Ver la carta". On load the bays' green shutters roll up once.

FORM: Painted shopfront and sign-writing, grounded candidate 5 of 7 (dealt by the roll). seed:be694f7519bc8068. Raises from declined challengers: Japanese high-density web → the menu is one dense board holding all 13 items in about one and a half screens; blacksmith forging → the ritual registers true measurements (25 s, 65 °C) as large numerals; HyperCard stack → every menu category is a deep link with its own index; night-flight six-pack → each section owns one truth, no fact repeated.

SIGNATURE: The shutter roll-up on load (once; skipped under reduced motion) and the ritual section's sticky photograph that changes with each step.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Scope

Landing page (single route). Visitor mode: Persuade. Audience: people curious about a calm
neighbourhood café, mostly on phones; portfolio reviewers. Action: contact the café (WhatsApp,
Instagram, email — demonstration channels, visibly labelled). No prices, no ordering, no forms.

## Direction contract

REPLACES: "The Painted Shopfront" (bottle green + gilt sign-writing). Replaced on the user's
request of 2026-11-19: the palette fought itself (green, gilt and the photos' amber), the sign
lettering read dated, photographs sat in frames inside frames, sections shared one template and
the contact block overlapped at tablet widths. The old look is evidence, not authority.

THESIS: The page is Monika's house recipe card, read as an editorial: the café's craft written in
its own notation — doses, seconds, degrees, what goes into each cup — set beside large, quiet
photographs. Read top to bottom, the page is a cup in section: foam, milk, crema, espresso.

OWN-WORLD: Grounds are the layers of a coffee: milk white #FCFAF7 (foam: header, hero, the house),
cream #F5EFE6 (milk: the menu), crema #E4CDAE (crema: the ritual), espresso #241812 (the room,
contact, footer). Ink is espresso brown; the one accent is caramel #C9A27A, used on espresso only.
Brygada 1918 (a 1918 book face, roman and italic) sets headlines, the wordmark and the
measurements; Familjen Grotesk sets text, labels and notation. Hairline rules like a ruled card.
Photographs are square-cornered, unframed, colour-graded as one set (warm, matte, lower
saturation).

STORY: In the first seconds the visitor sees the room in afternoon light, reads what Monika's is on
a recipe card set over the photograph, and finds "Escríbenos". Scrolling goes down the cup: the
house, the whole menu with what goes into every drink, the four measured gestures behind each cup,
the room at any hour, then the door: three channels.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A wide photograph of the room fills the top; a milk-coloured recipe card overlaps its
lower-left edge holding the h1, one sentence, "Escríbenos" and "Ver la carta"; beside it, under
the photograph, a ruled notation line (18 g · 25 s · 65 °C) linking to the ritual. Not a split hero, not a headline over cards.

FORM: assigned grounded candidate 6 of 7 (seed: seed:c61a2f7a0b3a4971), the barista's
recipe card, fused with the brief's pinned "contemporary editorial café". Challengers dealt:
darkroom safelight, minihompy room, synth horizon, torn flyer wall, bioluminescent wake, night
cityscape — all declined on audience identification and product clarity.

RAISES:
- From the night cityscape (declined): the page passes through light states. Here the grounds
  move from foam to espresso as the visitor scrolls, with soft seams between the light grounds
  and a clean edge into espresso, so no section stands alone.
- From the darkroom (declined): stations in a strict, timed order. The ritual is four numbered
  stations on one timeline that draws as it is read; order carries the information.
- From the torn flyer wall (declined): scale contrast with conviction. Measurements and headlines
  at display size against small notation, never a middle size between them.

SIGNATURE: The cup in section — every drink on the menu carries a small cup drawn in layers of
what goes into it (espresso, milk, foam, ice…), filling as it scrolls into view; the hero
photograph fills the same way on load. MOTION GRAMMAR: one family of exponential ease-out; things
fill from the bottom like a poured cup; hover is quiet (2–3% photo zoom, arrow nudge); nothing
waits on scroll to become readable; everything rests under prefers-reduced-motion.

FINISH: Every section a different composition inside one system; no eyebrows, no card grids, no
frames inside frames; contact and footer laid out from content, never from viewport-scaled single
words; browser surfaces (selection, focus, scrollbar) themed from the palette.

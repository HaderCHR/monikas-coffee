## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static site built with Astro (zero client JS by default, build-time image pipeline,
components for repeated catalog markup). Chosen because the brief leaves the stack open and asks
for something modern, stable, maintainable and dependency-light.

## Users

- People curious about a neighbourhood café: deciding whether it is a place they would like to
  sit in, what it serves, and how to ask about it. Mostly on phones. **(inferred)**
- Reviewers of a web portfolio (clients, recruiters) judging design and engineering quality.

## Product Purpose

Monika's Coffee is a **fictional** café used as a portfolio and demonstration project. The site
presents it as a warm, calm, quality brand and invites the visitor to get in touch to ask about
products, prices or availability. Success: the visitor enjoys the presentation, understands what
the café offers, and finds the contact channels without effort.

## Positioning

A café built around a calm pause: carefully made coffee drinks and house pastries in a quiet room
with natural light. No sales platform, no ordering.

## Operating Context

A one-page landing site: navigation, hero, presentation of the café, visual catalogue without
prices, the experience and its details, an atmosphere gallery, contact and footer.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- No cart, ordering, payments, sign-in, accounts, backend, database or admin.
- No form that pretends to send anything without a real service.
- No prices anywhere.
- Contact channels (WhatsApp, Instagram, email) are demonstration data and must be visibly
  labelled as such; links must not pose as working real destinations.
- No invented real-world facts: no street address, opening hours, ratings, reviews, exact
  location, business identifiers. Structured data must not make the café look like a verifiable
  business.
- Catalogue (brief): Cafés clásicos (espresso, americano, cappuccino, café latte); Bebidas
  especiales (mocha, vanilla latte, iced caramel latte); Bebidas frías y alternativas (iced latte,
  matcha latte, té frío de frutos rojos); Repostería (croissant de mantequilla, roll de canela,
  cheesecake). Each item: name, photograph, short description.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Monika's Coffee.
- Personality: welcoming, natural, warm, calm, elegant; editorial presentation, not a template.
- Orientative palette from the brief (may be refined or extended): cream #F5EFE6, dark brown
  #4A3026, coffee brown #8B6B55, warm grey #77716B, warm white #FCFAF7.
- Language: Spanish, neutral register, informal "tú". **(inferred)**

## Evidence on Hand

- No logo, no client photographs, no real contact data, no address, no hours.
- All photography is licensed stock used as stand-ins (listed with credits in README.md).
- Nothing may be presented as a testimonial, review, rating, award or customer.

## Product Principles

1. Photographs carry the brand; every product is shown, each with its own picture.
2. Calm over loud: restraint in motion and decoration, generosity in space.
3. Honest demo: anything fictional that could be mistaken for real is labelled.
4. Contact is the one action that matters.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA as the working standard: contrast, keyboard access, visible focus, reduced motion,
touch targets of at least 44px. **(inferred)**
